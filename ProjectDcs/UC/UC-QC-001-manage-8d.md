## UC-QC-001: Manage 8D Quality Reports

### Primary Actor
Quality Manager / Quality Engineer

### Goal
Create, update, and close 8D quality reports to resolve quality issues

### Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|-------------|----------|
| Quality Manager | Overall quality system health |
| Production | Minimizes disruption from issues |
| Engineering | Root cause identification |
| Customer | Resolution of reported issues |

### Trigger
Quality issue identified (customer complaint, internal defect, audit finding)

### Main Flow

1. Quality issue reported
2. Quality Manager creates 8D
3. Team assembled (D1)
4. Problem described (D2)
5. Interim containment (D3)
6. Root cause analysis (D4)
7. Corrective action selection (D5)
8. Implementation (D6)
9. Prevention measures (D7)
10. Team recognition (D8)
11. 8D closed

### Alternative Flows

**A1: Urgent Issue**
- D1-D3 expedited
- Parallel D4-D5 work
- Validation within 24 hours

**A2: Reopened Issue**
- New evidence emerges
- Status reverted to 'In Progress'
- Additional actions required
